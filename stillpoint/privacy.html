Privacy Policy

Stillpoint: Meditation Bell for Mac

Effective September 23, 2026

In short: Stillpoint doesn't collect, send, or share any information about you. Your settings and meditation history stay on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. Stillpoint has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party code. It doesn't connect to any server of ours. The only network connection it makes is to Apple's App Store, through Apple's own software, when you buy or restore Stillpoint Pro.

What stays on your Mac

  • Settings: things like session length, bell sound, volume and reminder hours.
  • Meditation history: the date, time and length of each sit.

These are stored inside Stillpoint's own folder on your Mac and are never sent to us. Like other files on your Mac, they may be included in your own backups, such as Time Machine.

To delete sits, select them in the History window and press Delete. Deleting the app removes the app itself; to remove its data as well, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.poglesbyg.mindfulbell.

Purchases

Stillpoint Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts and Focus

If you use Stillpoint with Siri, the Shortcuts app, or a Focus filter, macOS passes your request to Stillpoint on your Mac. Stillpoint doesn't send it anywhere. Siri requests themselves are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Stillpoint collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
