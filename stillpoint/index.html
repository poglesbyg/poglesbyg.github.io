Stillpoint

A meditation timer and mindfulness bell in your Mac's menu bar

Need help? Email acorn.trek@gmail.com and include your macOS version.

Getting started

After you open Stillpoint, look for the bell in the menu bar at the top of your screen. Click it to choose a session length, optional interval bells, and a short settle-in time, then click Begin. One bell opens the sit and three bells close it. While you sit, the menu bar shows the time remaining.

Stillpoint needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Stillpoint Pro

The timer, interval bells and the singing bowl are free. A one-time purchase of Stillpoint Pro adds:

  • History, streaks, and minutes per day
  • Mindful Day: an occasional bell through the day to stop and breathe
  • The temple bell and small chime
  • Shortcuts actions, Siri, and Focus filters

Sits are saved even before you buy Pro, so your history is all there when you unlock it.

Restoring a purchase

On a new Mac, or after reinstalling, click Unlock Pro in the menu and choose Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Mindful Day

Turn on Mindful day in the menu to hear a single bell every so often, only during the hours you choose. Vary the timing makes each gap somewhere between half and one and a half times the interval, so the bell doesn't become background noise. The bells stay quiet while you're meditating, and any bell that came due while your Mac was asleep is skipped.

Shortcuts and Siri

With Pro, Stillpoint adds these actions to the Shortcuts app: Start Meditation (with an optional length), End Meditation, Ring Bell, Set Mindful Day, and Get Meditation Minutes. You can also say “Start meditating with Stillpoint” to Siri.

Turning on Do Not Disturb while you sit

macOS doesn't let apps change Focus modes themselves, so Stillpoint does it through two shortcuts you create:

  1. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut named Sit Focus On with the Set Focus action, turning Do Not Disturb on. Make a second one, Sit Focus Off, that turns it off.
  2. In Stillpoint, open Settings and enter those names under Run a shortcut.

The Shortcuts app may appear briefly when a sit begins and ends.

Focus filters

In System Settings › Focus, choose a Focus, then under Focus filters add Stillpoint. It can silence Mindful Day bells while that Focus is on, or start a sit when the Focus turns on.

Common questions

I don't hear the bell

Check the volume slider in Stillpoint's menu and your Mac's output volume and device. Click the bell button next to the slider to test.

Does Stillpoint keep my Mac awake?

During a sit, yes, so the bells ring on time. Your screen can still dim and lock. Closing the lid will still put a laptop to sleep.

Where is my data?

Only on your Mac. Stillpoint doesn't collect anything. See the privacy policy.
